`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic) writes one line to
`/output/entry.txt` — the text a gamer sends the boss to skip work on
GTA 6 launch week:

> Sorry, my boiler leaked overnight and the plumber can't come till four, I'm stuck home with a mop.

18 words, 98 characters: one line, plain text, no setup, no signature.
A boiler leak and a plumber who cannot come until four is the boring,
specific, almost-believable kind of excuse a tired manager has heard a
hundred times — and the mop is the detail that sells it.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. It self-checks the gate (1–140 characters,
at most 20 words, a single line) before writing.
